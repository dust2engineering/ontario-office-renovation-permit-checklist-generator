# Ontario Office Renovation Permit Checklist Generator - Ontario

**City:** Toronto | **Area:** 1800 sq ft | **Employees / occupants:** 18

> Preliminary checklist generated with the free open-source Ontario Office Renovation Permit Checklist Generator by Dust 2 Engineering Inc., Mississauga, Ontario. Verify every item with your designer and the local building department before submitting.

## 1. Application basics (always required)
- [ ] Municipal building permit application form, completed and signed by the owner or authorized agent
- [ ] Schedule 1: Designer Information form for each qualified designer on the project
- [ ] Owner authorization if the applicant is not the property owner, plus the lease for a tenant fit-out if the city asks for it
- [ ] Zoning review first: confirm the office use, floor area and any outdoor elements are permitted at this address
- [ ] Occupant load calculation shown on the floor plan, based on the actual layout

## 2. Architectural drawings
- [ ] Existing and proposed floor plans: open work area, private offices, boardrooms, reception, kitchenette and washrooms
- [ ] Occupant load from boardrooms and training rooms shown - meeting rooms often drive exit and washroom counts
- [ ] Barrier-free path of travel, barrier-free washroom and barrier-free boardroom access where required
- [ ] Fire separations and ratings for suite, corridor and exit walls shown on plan
- [ ] Reflected ceiling plan, door schedule and interior elevations as required by the city

## Server / IT room
- [ ] Dedicated cooling for the server room on the HVAC drawings, with heat load from the equipment list
- [ ] Electrical drawings showing UPS, dedicated circuits and panel capacity for the server room

## 3. Mechanical and electrical
- [ ] HVAC drawings: zoning for boardrooms and private offices, fresh air per occupant, washroom exhaust
- [ ] Electrical drawings: panel schedule, workstation power, lighting, exit signs and emergency lighting
- [ ] Fire alarm device layout where walls change; sprinkler head relocation shown where ceilings or walls move - sprinkler work by a licensed contractor

## 4. Other items (not part of the building permit)
- [ ] Landlord work letter and building rules: construction hours, freight elevator booking, base-building HVAC and sprinkler shutdown procedures
- [ ] Check whether your lease requires landlord approval of the drawings before the city application

## Before you submit
- [ ] One combined PDF set: architectural, mechanical, plumbing, electrical and structural (if any), all consistent with each other
- [ ] All drawing sets use the same address, unit number, floor area and counts
- [ ] Designs that require an engineer must be stamped by a licensed Ontario P.Eng.; BCIN designer information where applicable
- [ ] Book a pre-consultation or zoning check with the city if the use or layout is not clearly permitted
